Pr(t) maximum Ω²/Ω′²
Ω′/2π = √(Ω²+δ²)/2π
θ, axis from the pole
max Pr = sin²θ

The atom starts in |g⟩ and evolves under constant Ω and δ. The plot gives the height and the period of the oscillation; the geometry behind both is on the Bloch sphere. The state precesses about a fixed axis n ∝ (Ω cos φ, −Ω sin φ, −δ), tilted by an angle θ from the vertical |g⟩–|r⟩ axis, with tan θ = Ω/|δ|. The precession, and with it Pr(t), runs at the generalised Rabi frequency Ω′ = √(Ω² + δ²). Starting from the ground state |g⟩ at the south pole, the vector circles the axis on a cone of half-angle θ, so its farthest point lies at an angle 2θ from the pole. The maximum Pr = Ω²/(Ω² + δ²) = sin²θ follows from this geometry. With δ = 0 the axis lies in the equatorial plane and the cone passes through the Rydberg state |r⟩; with |δ| ≫ Ω the axis is almost vertical and the vector circles tightly around the pole. The phase φ only rotates the axis about the vertical: together with δ and the pulse duration it gives any single-qubit rotation.

H = (Ω/2)(cos φ σx − sin φ σy) − (δ/2) σz, the sign of φ as in Pulser, |g⟩ at the south pole, no decay. The dashed line through the sphere is the rotation axis; the thin circle is the trajectory over one period.
