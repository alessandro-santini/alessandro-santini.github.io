Each atom is driven resonantly to a Rydberg state at Rabi frequency Ω. Two Rydberg atoms interact with strength V = C6/R6, shown as V/2π in MHz. When V ≫ Ω the doubly excited state is off resonance: the pair shares a single excitation and oscillates at √2 Ω. The crossover sits at the blockade radius Rb = (C6/Ω)1/6.

Resonant drive, no decay, exact evolution restricted to the three states |gg⟩, |gr⟩ + |rg⟩ and |rr⟩. Each disk has radius Rb/2, so the two disks touch exactly at R = Rb. C6/ħ = 865 723 rad·µs⁻¹·µm⁶, the Pulser value for n = 60. Dimensionless view: lengths in units of rmin = 5 µm, energies in units of Jmax = C6/rmin6.
