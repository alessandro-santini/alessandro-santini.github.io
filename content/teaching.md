### Neutral Atom Computing in Practice

[EPITA](https://www.epita.fr/en/) (Le Kremlin-Bicêtre, Paris), Autumn 2026.

A short course for engineering students on how a neutral-atom quantum computer actually gets programmed. It moves from Rydberg atoms as qubits and the analog Hamiltonian to writing and emulating a first program, then to adiabatic protocols for optimisation problems and quench dynamics, and ends with a hands-on project. Every session has a practical part in Python with [QoolQit](https://pasqal-io.github.io/qoolqit/latest/), running on emulators.
